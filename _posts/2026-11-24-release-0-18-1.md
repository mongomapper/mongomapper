MongoMapper 0.18.1 has been released.

This release makes default_scopes thread-safe, warns instead of raising when a key clashes with an internal method, raises MongoMapper::NoRootDocument when saving an embedded document without a root, fixes a memory leak in the descendants tracker on modern Rails, reduces allocations when reading keys, and adds MongoDB 9.0 to CI.

Thanks to the many contributors!

See the [changelog](https://github.com/mongomapper/mongomapper/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md#0181---2026-11-24) for full details.
